Official Rules & Regulations | IronGrip Racing Code of Conduct

The Iron Grip Racing League (IGRL) is built on clean racing, strategy, and respectful competition. All drivers must follow these rules to ensure fair, safe, and competitive racing.

RACE SETTINGS

  • Race Length: 50%
  • Tire Wear: 2X
  • Fuel Consumption: 2X
  • Yellow Flags: Relaxed
  • Stages: ON
  • Black Flags: ON
  • Custom Setups: Allowed

CAR & TEAM SYSTEM

Drivers claim an active NASCAR number from Cup, Xfinity, Truck, or ARCA. Manufacturer must match the real-life team.

Hendrick Motorsports Chevrolet
Team Penske Ford
Joe Gibbs Racing Toyota

• Number must match claimed car

• Manufacturer must match team

• Custom designs allowed (realism optional)

DRIVING STANDARDS

Must Do:

  • Predictable racing line
  • Avoid aggressive divebombs
  • Avoid reactive blocking
  • Stay aware of surrounding cars
  • Respect other drivers' space

Not Allowed:

  • Intentional wrecking / Retaliation
  • Brake checking
  • Multi-lane blocking
  • Unsafe contact with lap cars

LAP CAR RULES

  • Yield to faster cars and leaders
  • Do not block leaders or winners
  • Maintain predictable lines
  • No sudden moves in front of lead cars
  • Courteous behavior is expected

PROCEDURES

  • Starts & Restarts:
  • No passing before start/finish line
  • Lane discipline on initial start
  • No lane jumping T1/T2 on Lap 1
  • Pit Road:
  • Observe pit speed limits
  • Enter and exit safely
  • Blend properly into traffic
  • Unsafe pit behavior = Penalties
  • Black flags are enforced

INCIDENTS

  • Intentional cautions prohibited
  • Avoid unnecessary contact
  • Maintain vehicle control
  • Admins review all incidents
  • Not all contact results in penalty
  • Drivers are responsible for situational awareness

COMMUNICATION

  • No yelling, arguing, or toxic behavior
  • Voice chat must remain race-focused
  • STAY IN MAIN VC UNTIL QUALY ENDS
  • • May move to separate channels for race
  • Harassment / Distraction = BAN

CHAMPIONSHIP

  • Traditional season points system
  • Most points at end wins title
  • NO PLAYOFF FORMAT
  • Consistency is rewarded
  • Clean racing is rewarded

ADMIN NOTES

  • Admin authority is final
  • No cheating or exploits
  • No unsportsmanlike behavior
  • Rules subject to update
  • Drivers will be notified of changes

PENALTY SCALE

Egregious or repeated violations may result in permanent league bans.

Pit Stop Adjustments

Tire Pressure

Adjust based on chart to shift handling. Reduce for overall grip, especially on short runs.

Wedge

Reduce if tight, increase if loose. Primary mid-race adjustment.

Fuel Weight

Less fuel increases nose weight, causing the car to feel significantly tighter.
