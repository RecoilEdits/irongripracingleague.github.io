IGRCC CODE OF CONDUCT

Built on clean racing, strategy, and respectful competition. All drivers must follow these rules to ensure fair, safe, and competitive racing.

RACE SETTINGS

  • Race Length: 50%
  • Tire Wear: 2X
  • Fuel Consumption: 2X
  • Yellow Flags: Relaxed
  • Stages & Black Flags: ON
  • Custom Setups: ALLOWED

TEAMS & CARS

Drivers claim an active NASCAR number from Cup, Xfinity, Truck, or ARCA. Manufacturer must match real-life team.

HendrickChevrolet
PenskeFord
GibbsToyota

PAINT SCHEMES: Number must match claim. Mfr must match team. Custom designs allowed (realism optional).

DRIVING STANDARDS

MUST DO:

  • Hold predictable racing line
  • Avoid aggressive divebombs or reactive blocking
  • Stay aware of surrounding cars
  • Respect drivers' space

NOT ALLOWED:

  • Intentional wrecking or retaliation
  • Brake checking
  • Multi-lane blocking
  • Unsafe contact with lap cars

LAP CAR RULES

  • Yield to faster cars and leaders
  • Do not block leaders or race winners
  • Maintain predictable lines
  • Avoid sudden moves in front of lead cars
  • Courteous behavior expected

STARTS & RESTARTS

  • No passing before the start/finish line
  • Maintain lane discipline on initial start
  • Do not jump lanes through T1 and T2 on Lap 1

PIT ROAD

  • Observe pit speed limits
  • Enter and exit safely
  • Blend properly into traffic
  • Unsafe behavior = Penalties
  • Black flags are enforced

CAUTIONS

  • Intentional cautions prohibited
  • Avoid unnecessary contact
  • Admins review all incidents
  • Drivers responsible for situational awareness

PENALTIES

  • Pass-through penalty
  • Position loss / Points deduction
  • Race suspension
  • League removal for repeated offenses
  • Egregious violations = Permanent Bans

COMMUNICATION

  • No yelling, arguing, or toxic behavior
  • Voice chat must remain race-focused
  • Stay in main VC until qualifying ends
  • Harassment or distraction = Prohibited

CHAMPIONSHIP

  • Traditional season points system
  • Driver with most points wins title
  • NO PLAYOFF FORMAT
  • Consistency and clean racing rewarded

Race Hard. Race Clean. Earn the win.

Admin authority is final on all incidents, penalties, or disputes. Cheating, exploiting game mechanics, or unsportsmanlike behavior is strictly prohibited. Rules may be updated; drivers will be notified.
